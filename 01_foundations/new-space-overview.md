# New Space Overview

## 1. Scope
“New Space” denotes the transition from low-volume, long-cycle satellite programs to **commercial, high-scale constellations** with fast iteration, mass manufacturing, and product-like service delivery.

## 2. Drivers
- reduced launch cost and higher launch cadence,
- high-volume satellite manufacturing,
- software-defined operations and automation,
- demand for ubiquitous connectivity (mobility and remote coverage).

## 3. Operational definition
A system is “New Space SatCom” in the engineering sense if it combines:
- **constellation scale** (coverage and capacity via multiple spacecraft),
- **software-defined operations** (automation and continuous improvement),
- **commercial service loops** (SLAs, product metrics, user device integration).

## 4. Implications for 5G/6G
New Space systems increasingly align with mobile architectures:
- satellite as backhaul/transport,
- satellite as access (including D2D),
- hybrid multi-orbit and terrestrial integration.
