# Handoff Frequency

## 1. Why handoff dominates user experience
In LEO, “cells move.” Sessions may require frequent handovers due to satellite motion and beam scheduling.

## 2. Primary drivers
- altitude (lower altitude → faster apparent motion),
- beam footprint and scheduling,
- elevation mask and usable window definition.

## 3. Required metrics
- handoff rate distribution,
- handoff failure rate,
- continuity strategy (messaging vs voice).
