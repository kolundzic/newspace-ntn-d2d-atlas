# Ops Playbook (Starter)

## 1. Objectives
- maintain service continuity where required,
- maintain safety via SSA and maneuver discipline,
- maintain trust via evidence and transparency.

## 2. Minimal operational telemetry
- availability by region (time series),
- handoff success rates,
- delivery time distributions (p50/p95/p99),
- gateway health and latency,
- anomaly logs and postmortems.

## 3. Governance
- keep change logs (what/when/why),
- publish known limitations,
- separate marketing claims from evidence-backed claims.
