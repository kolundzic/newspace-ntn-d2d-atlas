# SSA & Conjunction Screening

## 1. Problem
Mega-constellations increase close-approach probabilities; safety depends on SSA workflows and timely screening.

## 2. Operational view
SSA systems support:
- object detection,
- orbit estimation,
- conjunction prediction,
- screening outputs (e.g., CDM-like artifacts).

## 3. Engineering relevance
- affects maneuver planning and uptime,
- impacts regulatory compliance and sustainability,
- drives operational risk profiles.
