# Ecosystem Snapshot (Starlink / AST / Lynk / Apple)

This chapter provides directional notes as a starting point, not a full market survey.

## 1. LEO broadband systems
Large-scale LEO broadband constellations tend to evolve toward:
- direct-to-cell extensions,
- hybrid routing and gateway strategies,
- advanced operational tooling (including SSA integration).

## 2. Direct-to-phone pioneers
AST and Lynk are frequently cited as “direct-to-phone” approaches emphasizing large apertures and carrier partnerships.

## 3. Consumer-device integrated services
Smartphone-integrated satellite services prioritize accessibility and user experience, often starting with emergency and messaging.
