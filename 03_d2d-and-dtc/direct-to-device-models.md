# Direct-to-Device Models

## 1. Definition
D2D/DTC describes satellite connectivity to consumer devices without dedicated sat terminals.

## 2. Simplified service model taxonomy
| Model | Mechanism | Typical initial capability |
|---|---|---|
| “Space base-station” | satellite presents cellular-like interface | messaging/data; evolving |
| Store-and-forward | delay-tolerant messaging | SOS/messaging |
| Assisted hybrid | terrestrial + satellite split | improved robustness |
| IoT variants | narrowband endpoints | telemetry/monitoring |

## 3. Engineering implications
- spectrum strategy and regulatory framing are central,
- availability and continuity define user experience,
- device constraints dominate uplink feasibility.
