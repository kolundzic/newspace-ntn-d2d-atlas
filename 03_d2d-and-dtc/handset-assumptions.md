# Handset Assumptions & Constraints

## 1. Rationale
D2D feasibility is often mischaracterized when handset constraints are not explicit.

## 2. Baseline constraints (qualitative)
| Constraint | Typical reality | Engineering consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Antenna aperture | small | low gain, higher margin requirements |
| TX power | battery limited | uplink is frequently the limiting direction |
| Orientation | arbitrary | beam management must tolerate variability |
| Mobility | variable | Doppler and handoff handling required |
| User behavior | intermittent | availability must be treated probabilistically |

## 3. Conclusion
D2D systems frequently embed complexity in the satellite payload and beamforming so that the handset remains “ordinary.”
