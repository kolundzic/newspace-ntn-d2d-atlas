# Walker Delta vs Walker Star (Polar) Constellations

## 1. Problem statement
A constellation design must satisfy **coverage**, **capacity**, and **handoff stability** under orbital and ground-segment constraints.

## 2. Definitions
- **Walker Delta**: orbital planes distributed over 360° of longitude (common for latitude-optimized patterns).
- **Walker Star / Polar**: near-polar inclinations with planes effectively distributed over 180° (strong polar coverage).

## 3. Qualitative comparison
| Property | Walker Delta | Walker Star (Polar) |
|---|---|---|
| Mid-latitudes | Strong | Strong |
| Polar coverage | Limited unless high inclination | Excellent |
| Handoff pattern | Latitude-dependent | More globally uniform |
| Gateway strategy | Region-optimized | More global distribution |

## 4. Engineering implication
Constellation geometry drives:
- visibility windows,
- handoff frequency,
- gateway placement economics,
- availability statistics by latitude.
