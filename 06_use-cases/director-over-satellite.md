# Director Over Satellite (Pilot)

## 1. Definition
A pilot scenario where a voice-first agent/device maintains communication over satellite links when terrestrial coverage is absent or degraded.

## 2. Constraints
- intermittent availability windows,
- service tier differences (messaging vs voice vs data),
- power constraints for field hardware.

## 3. Measurement outputs
- time-to-first-contact,
- availability window length statistics,
- end-to-end latency distribution,
- delivery success rates.

## 4. Next step
Formalize as a separate pilot package once the measurement plan and evidence schema are stabilized.
